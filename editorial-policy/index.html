Editorial Policy

Last reviewed: October 2026

Scope of content

Tips for Everyday covers routine household topics across six areas:

  • Home cleaning, laundry, appliance care, and stain removal
  • Household cost reduction (utilities, groceries, home maintenance)
  • Home organization and storage
  • Kitchen and food storage practice
  • Routine home repair and seasonal maintenance
  • Seasonal and holiday preparation

We deliberately exclude medical and health advice, investment and financial planning advice, legal advice, and any content requiring a professional license to dispense. If a household problem falls outside routine maintenance — structural work, gas appliances, main electrical panels, or anything requiring a permit — our guides say so and direct readers to a licensed professional.

How guides are produced

  1. Topic selection. Topics are selected based on recurring real-world problems, not on search volume alone. A topic must have a demonstrable, specific answer.
  2. Sourcing. Methods are compiled from manufacturer instructions, published consumer and safety guidance, and standard household practice. Where a figure or standard is cited, its source is named.
  3. Specificity requirement. Every guide must include concrete quantities, ratios, temperatures, or timeframes. A guide that can only say "use a cleaner" does not meet our standard and is not published.
  4. Limits disclosure. Every guide states what the method does not work on and where it can cause damage.
  5. Safety review. Guides involving chemicals, heat, electricity, or water must state the relevant hazards explicitly — including combinations to avoid (for example, never mixing vinegar and bleach).
  6. Update review. Published guides are reviewed periodically. Substantive changes update the "Last Updated" date shown on the article.

Sourcing and citations

Where we cite a figure, standard, or institutional recommendation, we reference the source in the text. Where a claim is based on commonly recommended practice rather than a published standard, we present it as such. Where evidence is limited or contested, we say so explicitly rather than stating it as settled fact.

Corrections

We correct errors when they are identified. If you believe a guide contains an inaccuracy, please contact us through the Contact page and include the article URL and the specific issue. Substantive corrections are noted by updating the article's "Last Updated" date. We do not silently remove content to hide errors.

Advertising and independence

This site is funded by display advertising served through third-party networks. The following rules apply without exception:

  • Advertisers have no input into editorial content, topic selection, or conclusions.
  • We do not publish sponsored guides presented as editorial content.
  • Advertising is clearly separated from editorial content and labeled where required.
  • Where affiliate links are used in the future, they will be disclosed on the page where they appear.

Use of automated tools

We may use software tools for drafting assistance, structure, and consistency checking. All published content is reviewed against the standards above before publication, and the specificity, limits, and mechanism requirements apply regardless of how a draft was produced. Guides that do not meet the standard are not published.

Disclaimer

Content on this site is general information for a general audience. It does not account for your specific materials, equipment, warranty terms, local building codes, or circumstances. Always test any method on a small hidden area first, follow the product manufacturer's instructions, and consult a licensed professional where appropriate.

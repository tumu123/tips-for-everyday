About Tips for Everyday

Tips for Everyday publishes practical guides for everyday household problems — cleaning, organizing, small repairs, and cutting household costs. Every guide is written to be followed, not skimmed.

What makes our guides different

Most household advice online stops at the general idea: "use vinegar," "clean the filter," "lower your thermostat." That is not enough to actually do the job.

Our guides are built around three requirements:

  1. Specific quantities and times. Ratios, temperatures, dwell times, and dilution levels — not vague instructions.
  2. Explicit limits. What the method does not work on, which surfaces to avoid, and when to call a professional instead.
  3. The mechanism. A short explanation of why a method works, so you can reason about substitutions instead of following steps blindly.

How we research and write

Our guides are compiled from product manufacturer instructions, published guidance from consumer and safety organizations, and commonly recommended household practice. Where a claim depends on a figure or standard, we note the source. Where evidence is thin or contested, we say so rather than presenting it as settled.

We do not accept payment in exchange for positive coverage, and we do not publish sponsored guides presented as editorial content. Advertising on this site is served through third-party networks and is clearly separated from editorial content.

What we are not

This site is not a substitute for professional advice. We do not provide medical, legal, financial, or licensed trade advice. Guides involving plumbing, electrical work, or structural repair are limited to routine maintenance that a homeowner can reasonably perform; anything beyond that should be handled by a licensed professional. See our Editorial Policy for the full scope of what we cover.
